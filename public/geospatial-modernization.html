The challenge

Modernization is a system change, not a software upgrade.

Organizations often reach a point where the GIS still works, but the cost of keeping it working keeps rising. Services accumulate, environments drift, scripts age, access patterns become harder to explain, and operational knowledge lives with too few people.

SpatialX approaches modernization as an architecture and operating-model problem. The goal is to understand what the organization depends on, protect what still creates value, reduce avoidable risk, and create a phased path toward a more secure and maintainable geospatial environment.

Common situations

  • Aging enterprise GIS platforms with unclear dependencies.
  • Upgrade or migration pressure without an agreed risk model.
  • Development, test, and production environments that have drifted apart.
  • Manual deployment, validation, reporting, or recovery practices.
  • Cloud or hybrid ambitions without a clear geospatial architecture.
  • Modernization programs that need to remain understandable to GIS, IT, security, and business stakeholders.

What SpatialX helps with

  • Current-state platform, service, data, identity, integration, and operations assessment.
  • Target-state architecture and modernization sequencing.
  • Service rationalization and dependency mapping.
  • Environment strategy, migration patterns, validation, cutover, and rollback planning.
  • Governance, monitoring, security, and operational-readiness requirements.
  • A roadmap that distinguishes immediate stabilization from longer-term modernization.
