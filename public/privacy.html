Privacy

Privacy and contact information.

A plain-language privacy note for SpatialX Solutions Inc.

Overview

SpatialX Solutions Inc. keeps website privacy simple. This website is intended to provide information about geospatial modernization services and make it easy for organizations to start a conversation.

Information you provide

If you submit a contact form or email SpatialX, you may provide your name, organization, email address, area of interest, timeline, and a description of what you are trying to solve.

How information may be used

Information you provide may be used to respond to your inquiry, understand your organization’s needs, schedule a conversation, and improve how SpatialX explains its services.

Contact form handling

Contact-form submissions are received by the SpatialX website and may be stored on the hosting environment so the inquiry can be reviewed and answered. When email notification is configured, the same inquiry may also be forwarded to SpatialX by email. Contact information is not collected for sale to third parties.

Analytics and website improvement

This website may later use basic analytics to understand page visits and improve content. Analytics should be configured in a privacy-conscious way before launch.

No phone number

SpatialX does not list a public phone number on this version of the website. The primary contact method is email: info@SpatialXSolutions.com.

Review note

This privacy page is a practical starter version and should be reviewed before publishing if legal, procurement, or client requirements require a formal privacy notice.
