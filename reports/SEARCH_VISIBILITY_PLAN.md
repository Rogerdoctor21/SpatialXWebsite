# SpatialX Search Visibility Plan — v4

## Objective
Build multiple legitimate discovery paths into SpatialX without diluting the premium geospatial-modernization brand or creating thin SEO doorway pages.

## Canonical brand
- **Primary domain:** spatialx.ca
- **Legacy/defensive domain:** spatialxsolutions.com → 301 to matching spatialx.ca URL
- **Brand line:** Geospatial modernization for secure, intelligent systems.

## Funnel strategy

### Problem search
Examples: “ArcGIS Server slow”, “GIS data quality issues”, “how to modernize ArcGIS Enterprise”.

Use Insights and future problem-specific articles to answer the immediate question and link naturally to the relevant capability page.

### Solution search
Examples: “GIS architecture Canada”, “secure GIS architecture”, “spatial data engineering Canada”.

Dedicated search-intent pages provide detailed problems, architecture response, deliverables, questions, and related capabilities.

### Vendor-selection search
Examples: “geospatial modernization consulting Canada”, “GIS consultant Canada”, “ArcGIS Enterprise consultant Canada”.

Homepage, Capabilities, Case Studies, About, structured data, external references, LinkedIn, and backlinks build company-level authority.

## What is intentionally not done
- No duplicate Toronto/Milton/province doorway pages.
- No attempt to rank two SpatialX domains independently.
- No fake “best GIS consultant” content.
- No fabricated case-study metrics.
- No keyword stuffing in the homepage hero.
- No AI-first rebrand.

## Off-site work required after launch
Technical SEO cannot create authority alone. After deployment:
1. Verify `spatialx.ca` in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
2. Submit `https://spatialx.ca/sitemap.xml`.
3. Request indexing for the homepage, Capabilities, Case Studies, and the new search-intent pages.
4. Build the SpatialX LinkedIn company page and connect it once it has credible content.
5. Publish Insights consistently and distribute each through LinkedIn / professional networks.
6. Earn legitimate references and backlinks through partnerships, industry participation, procurement profiles, speaking, articles, and relevant Canadian geospatial communities.
7. Add measurable case-study outcomes only when they are defensible and publishable.

## Success should be measured by
- Indexed pages, not just pages created.
- Search impressions by intent cluster.
- Growth in non-branded search queries.
- Qualified contact submissions / calls.
- Queries that move from impressions → clicks → conversations.
- Whether the right audience is discovering SpatialX, not raw traffic alone.
