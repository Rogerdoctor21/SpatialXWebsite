# SpatialX SEO / GEO QA Report — v4

## Scope
This iteration extends the existing v3 technical SEO foundation with a buyer-intent search architecture while preserving the current brand and design system.

## Technical SEO
- Canonical domain: `https://spatialx.ca`
- Legacy domain redirect: matching-path 301 from `spatialxsolutions.com`
- Unique canonical tags across all pages
- Unique titles and meta descriptions
- Open Graph and social preview metadata
- Structured data on all indexable pages
- Sitemap expanded to all 24 indexable URLs
- `robots.txt` retains standard crawler access and OAI-SearchBot allowance
- Privacy and 404 remain non-indexable
- Six Insight articles remain included in sitemap

## New search architecture
11 substantive pages were added. Each has a distinct buyer intent, unique page copy, separate deliverables/questions, Service structured data, breadcrumb schema, internal links, and canonical URL.

Content QA shows the new pages are roughly 400–460 words of visible primary content each, with low pairwise sequence similarity (maximum observed around 0.27 in the content QA), reducing the risk of near-duplicate doorway content.

## Internal architecture
- New pages are visibly linked from Capabilities and Industries.
- Each search page links to related capabilities and the Geospatial Platform Assessment.
- No hidden orphan-page strategy is used.
- No location-duplicate pages were created.

## Conversion
A Geospatial Platform Assessment is now positioned as a practical first engagement: current state, material risks, target direction, and phased roadmap. The open-ended Strategy Call remains available.

## Brand QA
The external reviews largely validate the intended position: enterprise strategy + technical rigor + architect-led delivery + low-hype tone. No major rebrand is recommended.

The current line **“Geospatial modernization for secure, intelligent systems.”** remains stronger for SpatialX’s Canadian direction than reverting to the older “Architecting Intelligent Geospatial Systems” wording because modernization, security, and operational trust are central to the present market position.

## Accessibility / UX
The existing v3 focus states, reduced-motion handling, responsive grids, sticky-header scroll margins, and dark/light contrast improvements remain intact. New pages use the same established component system.

## Known launch dependencies
- Final official logo is still pending.
- LinkedIn link intentionally removed until the SpatialX page has real content and a final URL.
- Strategy-call scheduling link can replace the contact anchor in a future iteration.
- Search rankings cannot be guaranteed by on-site work; indexing, authority, distribution, and backlinks remain necessary.
